Privacy Policy & Clinical Data Governance

PID Registry of Pakistan — Pakistan Society for Primary Immunodeficiency Disorders (PSPID)

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  |  Policy Version: 1.0.0

Intended Users: PID Registry of Pakistan is a clinical documentation, epidemiological registry, and educational decision-support application intended strictly for verified healthcare professionals (immunologists, pediatricians, hematologists, and authorized hospital staff aged 18+). It is not a consumer self-diagnosis app.

1. Regulatory & Ethical Alignment

PSPID operates this registry in accordance with national and international clinical governance and data protection principles:

  • Pakistan Legal & Bioethical Frameworks: Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016; National Bioethics Committee (NBC) Pakistan and institutional IRB/ERC biomedical research standards; Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PMDC/PMC) medical confidentiality rules; Provincial Healthcare Commissions record standards; and proactive alignment with the draft Pakistan Personal Data Protection Bill (MoITT) principles for sensitive health data and child protections.
  • International Clinical & Privacy Standards: EU GDPR (Article 9 special-category health data, data minimization, pseudonymization), HIPAA Security Rule technical safeguards, WMA Declaration of Helsinki, CIOMS International Ethical Guidelines for Health-Related Research, and ESID/IUIS registry stewardship standards.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

Category Data Elements Purpose & Custody
Clinician Account Data Full name, official email, phone number, hospital/center affiliation, PMDC/PMC registration number, designation, specialty, and Terms acceptance timestamp. Identity verification, role-based access control (RBAC), hospital scoping, and medicolegal audit accountability.
Patient Clinical Registry Data (PHI) Patient demographics, district/province, consanguinity/family pedigree, clinical red flags, hematology/immunology/flow cytometry results, molecular genetic variants, follow-up visits, and documented parent/guardian informed consent. Clinical care continuity, hospital cohort management, and strictly de-identified national epidemiological research. Patient records remain under the custody of the enrolling hospital.
Security & Audit Metadata Local SHA-256 salted PIN hash (stored on device only), login sessions, and clinical action audit timestamps. Device PHI protection, preventing unauthorized access, and clinical non-repudiation.

Zero Advertising or Data Brokerage: We never sell, rent, or transmit clinician or patient data to advertising networks, marketing platforms, or commercial third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Informed Consent & Pediatric Protections

Because Primary Immunodeficiency (Inborn Errors of Immunity) predominantly affects infants and children, participating clinicians must obtain and document informed consent from a parent or legal guardian prior to enrolling any minor into the registry, in line with NBC Pakistan, IRB/ERC, and Declaration of Helsinki standards. Parents/guardians may separately opt in or opt out of de-identified international consortium sharing (e.g., ESID/IPOPI) and biobanking.

4. Data Security, Offline Storage & Hospital Isolation

  • Transport & Storage Encryption: All cloud synchronization uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Optional local backup files (.pidbak) are encrypted with AES-256.
  • Strict Multi-Hospital Isolation: Database Row-Level Security (RLS) restricts clinical PHI strictly to authorized staff belonging to the enrolling hospital. Cross-hospital national analytics expose only aggregated, non-identifiable counts.
  • Device Protection: Optional 4-digit security PIN (stored locally as a salted SHA-256 hash) and device biometrics protect local access. Signing out or deleting an account automatically wipes locally cached SQLite patient records on the device.

5. Clinical Decision Support Disclaimer

Automated flags inside the app (including infant lymphopenia SCID alerts, delayed umbilical cord separation alerts, live-vaccine EPI contraindication references, and IUIS classification helpers) are clinical reference and educational aids only. They do not constitute a standalone medical diagnosis and must never replace qualified clinical judgment, specialist immunology consultation, or confirmatory laboratory/genetic testing.

6. Data Retention, Research De-Identification & Account Deletion

Research dataset exports include a de-identification mode that removes direct personal identifiers and generates a SHA-256 integrity checksum. Clinicians may permanently delete their personal account and credentials at any time inside the app (Settings → Delete Account & Personal Data) or online via our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page.

When a clinician deletes their account, their authentication credentials, personal profile (name, email, phone, PMDC number), and local device cache are permanently erased. Hospital-custodied patient medical records remain preserved with the treating institution under healthcare record-retention and NBC/IRB governance, with the departed clinician's personal account unlinked and anonymized in audit trails.
