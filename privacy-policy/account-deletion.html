Account & Data Deletion

PID Registry of Pakistan — Pakistan Society for Primary Immunodeficiency Disorders (PSPID)

This page allows authorized clinicians registered in PID Registry of Pakistan (developed by PSPID) to permanently delete their user account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open PID Registry of Pakistan on your Android or Windows device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account Card and tap Delete Account & Personal Data.
  3. Re-enter your password to verify your identity and confirm permanent deletion.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Action Upon Deletion Retention / Legal Basis
Clinician Login Credentials, Name, Email, Phone, PMDC/PMC Number, Designation Permanently Deleted (immediately in-app, or within 7 business days via web request) Not retained after deletion completes.
Local Device Cache (SQLite PHI & 4-Digit PIN) Wiped Immediately upon in-app deletion or sign-out Zero local retention.
Hospital-Custodied Patient Medical Records & Anonymized Audit Timestamps Unlinked from Your Personal Account (personal clinician identifiers scrubbed) Retained by the enrolling hospital under Pakistani healthcare record-keeping and NBC/IRB clinical governance requirements.
